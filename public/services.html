What We Offer

AgroVision provides AI‑driven cotton stress detection, environmental risk assessment, and actionable recommendations.

  • Real‑time visual stress classification using a CNN model.
  • Heatmap generation with Grad‑CAM for explainability.
  • Environmental data integration (temperature, humidity, soil moisture).
  • Customizable alerts and advisory messages.
