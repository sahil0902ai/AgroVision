DUAL-STREAM AI ENGINE

Four Integrated Intelligent Layers

Combining edge-optimized convolutional neural networks, biologically inspired spiking dynamics, deterministic agronomic rules, and large language model grounding.

Layer 1 · Computer Vision

Deep CNN Foliar Classification

A lightweight 110k-parameter Convolutional Neural Network trained on cotton canopy imagery. Classifies leaves into Healthy, Water Stress, Heat Stress, Nutrient Deficiency, and Pollution with individual softmax probability distributions and Grad-CAM saliency heatmaps.

  • Input resolution: 224×224 normalized RGB
  • Inference latency: < 25ms on CPU
  • Interpretability: Class Activation Maps (CAM)
Layer 2 · Spiking Neural Network

LIF Environmental Risk Screening

Simulates Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) biological neurons across 10 temporal timesteps. Encodes 33 environmental and agronomic features into temporal spike trains to gauge ambient field risk levels (Low, Moderate, High).

  • Membrane decay parameter: β = 0.95
  • Threshold: V_th = 1.0 with soft reset
  • Inference latency: < 2ms on edge hardware
Layer 3 · Decision Fusion

Deterministic Rule Arbitration

Cross-references visual leaf probabilities with environmental stress risk. Identifies localized microclimate variations (e.g. drip emitter clogging vs. regional drought) and triggers farmer-verified agricultural precautions.

  • Agronomic sanity checks preventing false alarms
  • Field-level alignment classification
  • Automated soil moisture & irrigation guidance
Layer 4 · Conversational AI

Gemini 2.5 Agronomic Explanation

Synthesizes model diagnostics, current field telemetry, and crop growth stage into plain-language actionable advice. Grounded strictly in verified scientific agronomy to prevent hallucinations.

  • Grounded in real-time sensor & model state
  • Farmer-friendly regional recommendations
  • Offline fallback agronomic engine built-in
